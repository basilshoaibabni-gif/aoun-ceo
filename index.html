03 / What's in an edit

What your listing gets

The same techniques from the series, on your footage.

  1. 00:00 – 00:01

    White flicker hook

    A flash cut in the first second so people stop scrolling before the house even shows.

  2. Door shots

    Door ripple

    The front door opens and the frame ripples into the next room. The tour feels like one walk.

  3. Room to room

    Speed ramps

    Fast through the hallway, slow on the kitchen. Bouncy ramps on the transitions.

  4. On screen

    Perspective & negative text

    Price, beds and area sitting on the wall or floor, tracked to the camera.

  5. You on camera

    Head tracking

    Text and graphics that follow you when you talk to camera.

  6. Whole video

    Colour grade

    Warm, bright interiors. Windows that don't blow out.

  7. Audio

    Sound design & music

    Snaps, whooshes and a track that fits the property.
